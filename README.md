# 📊 E-Commerce Sales Analytics

## 📝 Project Overview
This project involves analyzing e-commerce sales data to uncover insights related to product performance, customer acquisition channels, payment preferences, and seasonal revenue trends. The analysis helps in understanding customer behavior and evaluating the effectiveness of promotional strategies.

## 📁 Dataset
The analysis is based on the dataset provided in the file: `Product-Sales-Region.xlsx`.

## ❓ Business Questions Addressed
1. 💰 What is the total revenue generated across different product categories?
2. 📦 What is the breakdown of order statuses (e.g., delivered, cancelled, returned) for specific products?
3. 📢 Which marketing and referral channels are driving the most revenue?
4. 💳 What are the most frequently used payment methods by customers?
5. 📅 How does monthly revenue trend across different years (2023-2025)?
6. 🎟️ How do different discount coupons (e.g., FREESHIP, SAVE10) impact the total revenue across products?

---

## 💡 Key Insights

### 📦 Product Performance
* 🏆 The total revenue across all tracked categories is **1,264,761.96**.
* 🪑 **Chairs** (195,620) and 🖨️ **Printers** (195,612) are the top-grossing products, closely followed by 💻 **Laptops** (192,126).
* 📱 **Phones** generated the lowest total revenue (151,722) among the listed products.

### 🚚 Order Fulfillment (Chair Category)
* ❌ Analysis of the 'Chair' category reveals a high cancellation rate of **25%** (45 out of 178 orders).
* ↩️ Additionally, **16%** of chair orders were returned. This indicates potential issues with product quality, expectation mismatch, or shipping damages that need addressing. Only **19%** of the orders are currently marked as delivered.

### 📢 Customer Acquisition & Marketing
* 📸 **Instagram** is the most lucrative referral source, generating the highest revenue at **275,285**.
* 📧 **Email marketing** is the second most effective channel, bringing in **261,808**, proving that direct digital communication remains a strong driver of sales.

### 💳 Payment Preferences
* 🌐 Out of 1,200 total orders, **'Online'** payment is the most preferred method (258 orders), followed closely by 💵 **'Cash'** (246 orders).
* 💳 Other methods like Credit Card, Debit Card, and Gift Cards are evenly distributed, showing that customers utilize a diverse range of payment options.

### 📈 Revenue Trends
* 📉 The year **2023** saw a total revenue of 552,643, while **2024** saw a slight decrease to 480,235. 
* ⛰️ There are distinct monthly fluctuations, with a notable peak in **June 2024** reaching 68,068 in revenue.

### 🎟️ Promotion and Coupon Impact
* 🆓 The **'FREESHIP'** coupon generated the highest associated revenue (335,036), outperforming other discounts like 'SAVE10' (304,840) and 'WINTER15' (302,483).
* 🤔 Interestingly, revenue with no coupons applied ('NA') stood at 322,401, which is higher than the percentage-based discount codes, suggesting that free shipping is the most compelling promotional offer for these customers.
